A shield against pornography.

Magen blocks over 1 million adult websites across your whole Mac: every browser and every app. It doesn't slow the internet down and doesn't hide innocent photos of clothed people. When it blocks a site, it shows a Bible verse. Magen means “shield” in Hebrew.

How it works

Blocking

  • Before opening a site, your Mac asks for its address. Magen answers “doesn't exist” for adult sites.
  • Open lists: OISD NSFW, Block List Project and StevenBlack.
  • Everything else is looked up at Cloudflare (1.1.1.1).

Verses

  • 43 verses from the Bíblia Livre, each with a short title like “You can run from this.”
  • The button only works after a few seconds (3 to 15, your choice).
  • You can turn it off in the Verses tab.

Anti-bypass

  • Unblocking a site: sentence + password + 1-hour wait.
  • Sites with an obviously adult name can never be unblocked.
  • Removing Magen needs a sentence and the Mac password.

Honest limits

  • It blocks most adult sites, never 100%: new ones appear every day.
  • Anyone with the admin password can turn it off.
  • A VPN may get around it.

How to install

  1. Download Magen (a .dmg file).
  2. Open it and drag Magen to your Applications folder.
  3. Open Magen from Applications. The first time, your Mac says it can't verify the app, because it's free and not from the App Store. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom and click Open Anyway.
  4. Click Ligar a proteção (“Turn on protection”) and enter your Mac password. Done.

You need

  • A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 13 or newer.
  • The Mac's administrator password, to turn protection on.

Before turning it on

  • Turn off iCloud Private Relay (System Settings › your name › iCloud). With it on, some apps lose internet access. Magen warns you and helps.

Privacy

Magen has no account, collects no data and doesn't keep a list of the sites you visit. It only counts how many blocks happened, on your own Mac. It uses the internet to download the block lists once a day.
